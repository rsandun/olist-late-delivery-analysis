Operations should:
  • Focus on the five north-east states (AL, MA, PI, CE, SE), where up to 1 in 4 orders is late.
  • Track orders still in transit after their expected date, and investigate the slowest 10% of orders.
  • Put the top sellers by revenue at risk on a watch list.
  • Target: no order more than 3 days late. About 5,000 orders above that line carry about 90% of revenue at risk.
Marketing should:
  • Send an apology message and a small voucher to customers whose order will be more than 3 days late.
  • Test first: send it to half of the late orders, compare reviews and repeat purchases after 1 to 2 months.
  • The plan breaks even if it recovers about 14% of at-risk revenue.
Limits: Revenue at risk is not proven lost money. Recovery rates and voucher cost are assumptions. The data is old, and most customers buy only once.
